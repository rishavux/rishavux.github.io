01

EZ Brow & Beauty

Brand and UX system for a family-run salon

Role
Design Lead
Year
2026
Team
Solo

Problem

A salon that has served loyal clients for more than a decade had no digital presence equal to its standing. Its website drew little traffic, and its services sat in a flat menu with no hierarchy and no sense of value.

What I did

Wrote the brief from nothing, through market research and direct observation of the business. Built a complete brand and UX system (color, type, voice, information architecture, 30+ components) and redesigned the mobile service menu and cart, while keeping the booking flow returning clients already trust.

02

PropertyPixel

UX audit and redesign of an AI-native SaaS product

Role
UX Designer
Year
2026
Team
Solo

Problem

PropertyPixel turns property photos into listing-ready content. It was built by a solo founder, with little usage data and no design review. The landing page hid its proof, and the core studio had to handle batch work without overwhelming the user.

What I did

Audited the live product through PostHog session recordings, competitor benchmarks and usability heuristics. Made nine interface decisions across the landing page, studio, billing and demo: visible proof, distinct calls to action, labeled controls, clear selection and legible usage.
